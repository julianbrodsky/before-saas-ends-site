Before SaaS Ends

Questions, bugs, or a dinosaur who won't sign? Email jibbygames@proton.me and we'll get back to you. It helps to mention your device, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Quick answers

How do I drive? Walk to the wagon with your left thumb and tap Get in the car. Hold GAS, and drag the wheel pad on the left to steer. Prefer tilting? Turn on Tilt to steer in the menu.

How do I drift? At speed, hold BRAKE while steering hard. Hold a drift long enough for a burst of speed.

How do I talk to a dinosaur? Brake on the gravel pull-off beside them, tap Get out and say hello, walk up, and tap Talk.

I'm stuck. Open the menu and choose Tow me back to the road.

How do I start over? Open the menu and choose Start a new trip. Each new trip gets a new road.
