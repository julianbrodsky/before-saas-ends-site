Privacy policy

Before SaaS Ends. Last updated September 28, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Before SaaS Ends has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and never connects to the internet. It contains no third party code.

What stays on your device

Your trip (progress, deals, items) and your settings (sound, tilt steering, best times) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are included in your own device backups if you use those, and are deleted when you delete the app. To erase your progress without deleting the app, open the menu in the game and choose Start a new trip.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications. If you turn on Tilt to steer, the game reads the device's motion sensor to steer the car. Those readings are used only in the moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because no data leaves your device.

Money in the game

Sales figures in the game are fictional. There are no purchases of any kind.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.
